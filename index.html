About Me

Who I Am

I'm a Physical Science undergraduate at the University of Kelaniya with a passion for design, technology, and storytelling. Over 5 years of professional graphic design experience using Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop has sharpened my eye for detail, brand aesthetics, and visual communication.

From UI/UX mobile app prototypes, social media campaigns, and costume graphics to vector portraits and logo systems — I bring ideas to life visually. I also build web experiences using HTML, CSS, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, PHP & MySQL.

Currently serving as Chief Editor, Technical Head & Digital Creator of the Chemical Society at University of Kelaniya (promoted from Senior Editor & Technical Sub Head) and Vice President of the Education Subcommittee, Faculty of Science (promoted from Editor).
